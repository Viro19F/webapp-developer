Classic Visit & Tasting

The perfect way to discover Pinuaga. We walk the old vines, visit the cellar and the barrel room, and finish with a guided tasting on the terrace.

Includes:

  • — Guided tour of the vineyard and cellar (60 min)
  • — Tasting of 4 wines from the collection
  • — Manchego cheese and local olive oil pairing
  • — Hosted by a member of the family

Duration: 90 minutes · Group: 2 to 8 · Languages: EN · ES
